What can you do here?

Shopping online in dollars, euros or any other currency? This page tells you what it costs in shekels (NIS).

  • Convert any amount: type a number, pick a currency and see the result right away.
  • Go both ways: press ⇄ to switch between "NIS to foreign currency" and "foreign currency to NIS".
  • Check today's rates: the tiles below show what 1 unit of each popular currency is worth in NIS. Click a tile to use it in the converter.

Live online data. Rates come from the Apify “Exchange Rates” service, which collects current exchange rates from the web and refreshes them automatically every few hours. Nothing here is typed in by hand or taken from a spreadsheet.
